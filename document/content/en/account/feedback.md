---
title: "Feedback"
weight: 9
summary: "Submit feedback from the floating button; it becomes an issue."
---

When the administrator enables feedback, a floating **Feedback** button appears in the bottom-right corner:

1. Click it and describe your feedback (Markdown is supported).
2. On submit, gitdash uses the repository URL and token configured by the administrator to create an issue in that repository.
3. Signed-in submissions include the username and page URL to help triage.

Administrators configure the target repository and token under **Admin → User feedback**.
