---
title: "Repository settings"
weight: 9
summary: "Visibility, default branch, issues, template, topics, gc."
---

Under **Repository → Settings** you can manage:

- Visibility (public / private).
- Default branch.
- Issue tracker on/off.
- Template flag (used when creating repositories).
- Topics (used for Explore search).
- One-click `git gc`.
- Delete the repository.
