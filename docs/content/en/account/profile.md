---
title: "Profile"
weight: 1
summary: "Email, bio, avatar and notification preferences."
---

On the **Profile** page you can:

- Change your display email and verify it.
- Edit your bio (shown on your user page).
- Upload or remove an avatar (PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP, ≤2 MB; initials are shown otherwise).
- Toggle email notifications.

Changing your password revokes other sessions and keeps only the current one.
