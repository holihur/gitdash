---
title: "Two-factor authentication (MFA)"
weight: 5
summary: "TOTP and email one-time codes."
---

Enable MFA under **Profile → MFA**:

- **TOTP**: scan the QR code with an authenticator app and enter the 6-digit code to activate.
- **Email code**: a 6-digit code is sent to your email (requires SMTP).

Once enabled, signing in requires an extra verification step.
