---
title: "Create and merge a PR"
weight: 1
summary: "Open a PR, choose base/head, merge strategies and merge queue."
---

When opening a PR, choose the base (target) and head (source) branches and add a title and description. Before merging you can require:

- An approving review (see [Review](review/)).
- Passing status checks (for example CI).

Gitdash supports merge strategies (such as squash) and a merge queue for ordered merging.
